## Full description

**A customer messages you from a number you don't know. Now what?**
Save the contact… open WhatsApp… find the chat… type the same price reply you typed twenty times today. SellerChat deletes all of that.

**Paste the number. Tap once. The chat opens with your reply already typed.**

SellerChat is made for small online sellers — home-based shops, Instagram and Facebook sellers, resellers — who live on WhatsApp.

✓ **No saving contacts.** Paste any number and open a chat on WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business instantly. No more "who is this?" contacts filling your phone.

✓ **Your replies, ready.** Price inquiry, order confirmation, payment details, delivery update — one tap puts the template in the message box, with the customer's name filled in. You check it and press send. Nothing is ever sent automatically.

✓ **7 languages.** 24 ready-made seller templates in English, اردو (in beautiful Nastaliq script), Roman Urdu, Hinglish, Tamil, Telugu and Marathi — the languages you actually mix in a chat — and you can edit, reorder, and write your own.

✓ **Recents with labels — and a pipeline.** "Ayesha — kurta order", not a mystery number. Every customer gets a status (Inquiry → Ordered → Paid → Shipped), a waiting clock so no lead goes cold, a memo for sizes and delivery notes, and an optional follow-up reminder in 2 days — the notification opens that customer's chat directly.

✓ **Know your week.** A one-line dashboard shows your leads, who's still waiting, who's paid, and this week's paid total in your own currency.

✓ **Try it in one tap.** A demo button on the first screen opens WhatsApp with a sample chat, before you set anything up.

✓ **Your own order link + QR.** Generate your wa.me link and QR code, share it to your WhatsApp status, and customers land directly in your chat.

✓ **Works on both apps.** WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business are detected automatically — including dual-app phones.

**Private by design:**
Your numbers, labels, and templates stay on your phone. No account. No cloud. No contact access — nothing is asked when the app opens; the only permission prompt ever appears if *you* set a follow-up reminder (notifications — and the reminder still saves in-app if you say no). One small banner ad on the home screen keeps it free; there are never ads inside the chat flow.

Made for sellers in Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, the UAE, Nigeria, and everywhere WhatsApp is the shop counter.
